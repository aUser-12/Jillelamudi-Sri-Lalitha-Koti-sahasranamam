About the Divine Event

Participating in Sri Lalita Sahasranama Koti Parayana brings immense spiritual merit, harmony, and peace. Whether you join us physically at Jillellamudi, host a remote group in your city temple/mandali, or chant solo from your home, your recitations contribute to the sacred 10 Million goal!

"Where Lalita Sahasranama is chanted collectively with devotion, Divine Mother Mother's presence bestows health, prosperity, and peace."

Guidelines for Chanters

  • In-Person Dress Code: Traditional attire (Dhoti/Kurta for Men; Saree/Chudidhar for Women).
  • Count Reporting: All remote group coordinators and solo chanters are requested to send final count totals via WhatsApp by 06:30 PM on Dec 29.
